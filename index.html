This is the pre-record setup. Historical candle OHLC is fetched from Binance/API; the entry, final PNL, adverse drawdown, turn and take length are manual inputs, and the replay path is simulated from them under the SIM badge. Every ticker is locked to its completed 48-hour 15m series (192 candles). That full pool seeds the chart so its visible history is filled from the left edge; the actual candle shapes and direction sequence drive the record chart. Take length accepts 10 to 600 seconds and determines how many of those candles play back and how fast; the chart fills the selected take and keeps the live candle at the 45% reference boundary.

Daily workflow

  1. Pick a profile with ?account=1–5, or keep the saved one.
  2. Set ticker, direction, entry, final PNL, drawdown and turn seconds.
  3. Check the preview summary, then press Start recording.
  4. Start Android's screen recorder before pressing Start. The countdown holds the entry frame, then playback runs the full loss-to-target path.
  5. Record one take, reload, and repeat with a new real window.
The SIM badge stays on screen as the simulation marker. Presenting this replay as real account performance is misleading and may violate platform and financial-promotion rules.
